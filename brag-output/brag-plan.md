# brag-plan — A Day in Dhaka

**What:** a scroll-driven site that turns one vertical scroll into a full day in Dhaka (4:45 AM → midnight), one tea cup passed hand to hand.
**Angle (polished tone):** the real site, scrolled for you, from first light to midnight — scored with birdsong, because the day begins with birds.
**Hook (0–3s):** the real intro: "একটি দিন, ঢাকায" / A Day in Dhaka over the sunrise dot. Birds already on the pad.
**Highlights:** Azaan 4:45 → Old Dhaka chai 7:00 → The Rush 9:00 (fast sideways street) → Golden hour (pigeons) → Neon evening → Midnight tea.
**Punchline/outro:** tea cup at 23:45 dips to ink; "One scroll, one day." + share CTA.
**Visuals:** live page driven frame-by-frame by Playwright (real components, fonts, GSAP, shaders). Overlay only for end card.
**Sound:** D-major-pentatonic pad + synthesized birdsong tuned to the key (trills, whistles, two-note calls), light reverb; birds thin at dawn, full by golden hour, one soft chime resolve at end.
